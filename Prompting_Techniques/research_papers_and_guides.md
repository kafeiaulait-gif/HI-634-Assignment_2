# Definitive Research Papers & Surveys

* **The Prompt Report: A Systematic Survey of Prompting Techniques (2024)**
    * **Authors:** Sander Schulhoff et al. (University of Maryland, OpenAI, Microsoft, etc.)
    * **The Core Takeaway:** This is arguably the most comprehensive survey on prompt engineering to date. It establishes a formal ontology, outlining a vocabulary of 33 foundational terms, 58 distinct text-based prompting techniques, and 40 techniques for other modalities.
    * **Link:** View the full text on [arXiv:2406.06608](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.06608).

* **A Systematic Survey of Prompt Engineering in Large Language Models: Techniques and Applications (2024)**
    * **Authors:** Pranab Sahoo, Ayush Kumar Singh, Sriparna Saha, Vinija Jain, Samrat Mondal, Aman Chadha.
    * **The Core Takeaway:** This paper provides a highly structured overview categorized specifically by application areas. It details the exact methodologies, datasets, strengths, and constraints of advanced prompting paradigms.
    * **Link:** Available via [arXiv:2402.07927](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.07927).

* **Prompt Design and Engineering: Introduction and Advanced Methods (2024)**
    * **The Core Takeaway:** An excellent paper covering the architectural boundaries of LLMs (such as transient state and fabrication) and how techniques like ensembling, guardrails, and autonomous agent loops solve them.
    * **Link:** Read on [arXiv:2401.14423](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.14423).

## Leading Corporate & Academic Whitepapers

* **Google's Prompt Engineering Whitepaper (2025)**
    * **Overview:** A comprehensive, 68+ page deep dive mapping out the connection between model configuration parameters (like Temperature, Top-K, and Top-P) and practical text prompting. It highlights core patterns like *Step-Back Abstraction* and *Automatic Prompt Engineering*.
    * **Link:** YouTube · Dan Cleary +1

* **The OpenAI / DAIR.AI Prompt Engineering Guide**
    * **Overview:** A living, interactive repository that bridges academic literature with practical developer application, hosting individual breakdowns for over 15 major modern prompting formats.
    * **Link:** Explore the web guide at [PromptingGuide.ai](https://www.promptingguide.ai/).
